Who Uses It

  • Farmers and field workers planning crops or soil recovery.
  • NGOs targeting food-security and livelihood programmes.
  • Researchers comparing crop, soil and rainfall conditions.

Core Tools

  • SoilSense: soil health, erosion risk and recovery notes.
  • CropZone Mapper: crop suitability, NDVI and irrigation opportunity.
  • ADI map: province-level priority and data-gap screening.
