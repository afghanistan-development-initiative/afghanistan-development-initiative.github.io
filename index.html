Project Blueprint

What ADI can become

ADI is a public knowledge infrastructure project for Afghanistan: a place where open data, maps, policy briefs, dashboards and AI-supported guidance are connected into one practical system.

From scattered reports to usable decisions

Afghanistan development evidence is often split across PDFs, portals, donor reports, dashboards, and local knowledge. ADI brings those pieces together so a farmer, student, NGO, researcher, public institution, investor, or diaspora partner can start from one clear place.

The goal is not to replace field work. The goal is to help people know where to look first, what the evidence says, what is uncertain, and which tool or partner pathway should come next.

Target Groups

Who ADI is built to support

ADI should be useful for people who need evidence quickly, not only for technical specialists. Each audience gets a clear route into the tools, maps, briefs, and open datasets.

Farmers & field workers

Practical land, soil, crop and water decisions

Use ADI to understand local constraints, then connect detailed field recording and advisory workflows through ZaminAI where farmer-level data is needed.

  • Soil risk and recovery options
  • Crop suitability and irrigation potential
  • Dari, Pashto and English access pathway
NGOs & implementers

Programme targeting and monitoring

Compare provinces, identify evidence gaps, and connect interventions to open sources before designing field activities or donor proposals.

  • District and province prioritisation
  • Water, food security and livelihood evidence
  • Printable policy and method references
Government & municipalities

Policy briefs and national planning evidence

Use the platform as a neutral evidence base for agriculture, water, energy, economy, education and governance discussions.

  • Open indicators and source links
  • Non-political policy summaries
  • National-to-local map layers
Researchers & students

Reusable data, methods and research questions

Find open datasets, documented methods, and research notes that can be extended, reviewed, corrected or translated.

  • GitHub-first open source workflow
  • Methodology and source transparency
  • Contribution path for new studies
Funders & private sector

Evidence before investment

See where agriculture, energy, water and market opportunities are plausible, and where more ground validation is needed before committing resources.

  • Risk screening by sector

Diaspora & media

ADI was built from a simple observation — there is no open, reliable, data-driven platform covering Afghanistan's development challenges across all sectors. Everything is scattered, outdated, or behind paywalls.

Practitioners, researchers, and policymakers make critical decisions without the data they need. ADI is the attempt to change that — one tool, one dataset, one policy brief at a time.

This platform is built on field experience from FAO consultancy work in Afghanistan, combined with data science expertise developed across EU-funded projects and applied research in the Netherlands.

ADI is independent, non-political, and committed to open access. It welcomes collaboration from anyone who believes Afghanistan deserves better data.
